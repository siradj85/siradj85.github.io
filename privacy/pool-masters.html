Privacy Policy — Pool Masters

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Pool Masters ("the app") is a free offline 3D 8-ball pool game published by Siradj. This page explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information we collect

The app itself does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. Your game progress (wins, coins, unlocked cues and settings) is saved only on your device and is never sent to us.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob (rewarded videos you choose to watch for free coins, and occasional full-screen ads between matches). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and approximate location, in accordance with Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and the UK), a consent form is shown before any personalised ad is served. You can also opt out of personalised advertising at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" or "Delete advertising ID").

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to general-audience (G-rated) content.

Permissions

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — required only to load ads; the game itself works fully offline.
  • VIBRATE — for haptic feedback (can be disabled in the game settings).

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored game data. Since we do not hold any of your data on our servers, there is nothing further to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
